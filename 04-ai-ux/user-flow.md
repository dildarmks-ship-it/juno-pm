## Entry point

**Signal type:** New message / transcript received

A plan sponsor's benefit change request hits the account team intake queue (email or ticket) for a client whose executed contract is already loaded in Clara.

**What they see instantly**

"Clara is reading the request…" pill shows up on the ticket header right away. The Contract Fit and Impact panels on the right dim to about 40% so the account PM knows something is happening.

## The flow

1. RAG retrieval over the client's signed contract + benefit design exhibits (M3 KB), top-K = 6, latency target under 8 sec.
2. Clause mapping - each ask in the request gets matched to a contract clause and tagged In scope / Needs amendment / Out of scope.
3. Impact scoring - member disruption, adjudication config effort, revenue and rebate exposure.
4. Confidence check - score under 30 goes to "needs analyst review"; 70 and above auto-classifies with a clause citation.
2. "Pulling contract for Acme Health…" → "Matching 3 asks against 12 benefit exhibits…" → "Sizing implementation impact…"
3. Path A (contract loaded) → grounded classification with clause and exhibit citations.
Path B (contract missing or expired) → "Unverified mode": generic classification tagged low confidence plus a nudge to attach the executed contract before anything goes back to the client.

## AI moments

**Placement:** Inline & Embedded

One Contract Fit card per ask, sitting right on the ticket next to the request text. Each card has an In scope / Amendment / Out of scope badge, the client's quoted ask, the cited clause and exhibit number, an impact score, and a suggested next step (proceed, route to Contracts, or send back to client).

Augmentation (M2). The account PM is the validator - they edit and approve, they never draft the contract read from a blank page. Inline keeps the client's original wording on the left and the card on the right, so the PM can sanity-check the citation without leaving the ticket.

## Fallbacks

**Kill switch**

Every Contract Fit card has an "Override" button - the PM can re-classify, edit the cited clause, or mark it "Escalate to Legal". The suggested next step is editable, and nothing goes back to the client until a person hits Send.

**Training signal**

Any re-classification or clause edit is logged as a "contract-fit correction" against that client. 3+ corrections on the same clause flag it as ambiguous for the Contracts team and tighten retrieval for that client's exhibits.

**Fail-safe**

If no clause matches the ask, the card is tagged "Not covered by contract" with an amber warning. Clara never invents coverage. The PM can classify it manually or route it to the account executive, and the request stays in the queue - no dead end.
