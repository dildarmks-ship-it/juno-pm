# AI PRD · Juno

## Problem & user

RocketShip PMs need evidence-based prioritization. Juno turns noisy Slack, tickets, and strategy docs into a ranked, cited backlog they can defend.

## Solution overview

**Retrieval strategy:** Hybrid

We need the AI to find specific details (verify member coverage, driving history - look for prior accidents, review the photos submitted for the current accident) while also understanding broader context based on the chat history in the past (whether our customer could be at fault).

## Retrieval requirements (RAG)

- **Sources:** Progressive Auto Accident Claim Reviewer
* Accident Report pictures (JPEG/PNG) - upto 15 photos
• The Accident Claim Form (JSON)
• The police report - should be provided within 7 days of filling the accident report (PDF/PNG/JPEG)
- **Chunking / indexing:** Hybrid (Semantic + Keyword)
- **Grounding rule:** Verification: The AI cannot just give a Yes/No answer. It must site the evidence, photos, chat history, driving record, every decision points directly to the specific rule or photo it used.
Fail-safe: If the AI can’t find a clear reason to identify the member is at fault, it must stop and say “I don’t have enough evidence to decide,” and pass the case to a human. We prefer cautious over guessing.
- **Freshness:** Real-time trigger: The knowledge base must refresh its index instantly upon submission of a new accident claim
Requirement: To ensure accuracy, the “Member Coverage” and driving history and chat segment must be a live pull from the production database at the time of claim filing.

## Out of scope

Decisions that cannot be cited to a source in the knowledge base. Anything Juno is not allowed to retrieve or act on without a human.
