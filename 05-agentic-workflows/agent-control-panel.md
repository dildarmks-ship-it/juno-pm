## Autonomy level

Agent can only book the movie tickets if the total amount of booking is less than $200. For greater amount the agent needs permission from the PM

## Controls

- **Kill switch:** max_steps: 5. Abort if same tool fails 3 times in a row. Hard timeout: 90s.
- **Rate / cost caps:** BookingAgent.retrieve to {Movie:[{name,booked(boolean), confidence, score}], summary, showtime, dollar}}.
- **Escalate-on-stuck:** After 3 failed attempts to book the ticket provide an error message Booking cannot be completed at this time - try again later. For 3 failed attempts, unlock the booking flow and provide an error message that Payment gateway error was encountered - please try again later. In both the cases - send a detailed report to the PM on call

## Monitoring

**Confidence thresholds (map to actions):**

Payment details missing review with @on-call-pm. Payment more than $200 obtain approval from @on-call-pm. Booking for theater outside the mentioned miles (20), get approval from PM.

**Checkpoints:**

Any amount above $200 needs PM approvals. Any booking location outside of 20 miles radius needs PM approval to continue with the booking.

**North Star (re-read every loop):**

Do not book movie tickets more than $200. Do not book partial tickets. Do not book random seats. Do not book seats in the front 4 rows. Look for locations within 20 miles radius +- 5 miles

## Permissions

READ: Personal emails, files, payment details, Salesforce ARR. WRITE: #pm-daily only. CANNOT edit emails or payment details or post outside #pm-daily.
